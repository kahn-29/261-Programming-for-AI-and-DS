\section{Chosen Data \& Justification}
\label{sec:chosen-data}

The chosen dataset provides a large number of real-estate listings with a clear numeric target and a wide range of predictors, which suits the house price prediction task for the Vietnamese market.

\paragraph{Dataset}
\textit{Tinix Vietnam Real Estate Listings 2025--2026}~\cite{tinix_vietnam_real_estate_2025_2026}

\paragraph{Overview}
According to the published dataset card, the collection contains exactly 3{,}500{,}744 sale and rental listings published between June 2025 and March 2026, after a strict property-type filter, and covers all 63 provinces and cities of Vietnam. The listings are distributed as ten Parquet shards, are written primarily in Vietnamese, and are released under the CC BY-NC 4.0 licence for non-commercial academic use.

\paragraph{Justification}
The dataset contains over 3.5 million records, which supports stable exploratory analysis and supervised learning across regions and property types. Its schema separates a numeric target (\texttt{price}) from predictors describing location, property category, size and layout, orientation, textual content, and listing time. National coverage makes it possible to study regional price patterns and to train models that generalise beyond a single city, and the publication timestamp allows chronological train/validation/test splits, which are preferable to random splits for a market that changes over time. The publisher also lists real-estate price prediction among the intended use cases.
\paragraph{Limitations} The published definitions indicate that \texttt{price} may be undisclosed (\texttt{null}), that some structural fields are often incomplete for particular property types, and that the collection includes both sale and rental listings, with no variable that distinguishes them. These issues are examined in Section~\ref{sec:eda}, where an analytical subset of priced listings is constructed for subsequent analysis and model training.
