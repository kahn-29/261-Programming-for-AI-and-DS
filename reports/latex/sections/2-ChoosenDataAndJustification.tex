\section{Chosen Data \& Justification}
\label{sec:chosen-data}

\paragraph{Dataset}
\textit{Tinix Vietnam Real Estate Listings 2025--2026}~\cite{tinix_vietnam_real_estate_2026}.

\paragraph{Overview}
The collection contains 3{,}500{,}744 sale and rental listings published between June 2025 and March 2026 across all 63 provinces and cities of Vietnam, released under the CC BY-NC 4.0 licence for non-commercial academic use.

\paragraph{Justification}
The collection covers diverse property types and regions across Vietnam, providing a rich set of physical attributes, hierarchical locations, and unstructured descriptions. Its publication timestamps allow chronological splits that reflect realistic valuation scenarios (predicting future prices from past listings).

\paragraph{Limitations}
Key challenges include undisclosed asking prices, missing values in optional structural attributes (e.g., frontage, road width), the mixture of sale and rental advertisements without an explicit label, and self-reported listing errors. Section~\ref{sec:eda} examines these issues and constructs the cleaned analytical subset used for modelling.
