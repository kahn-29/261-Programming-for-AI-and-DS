\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study compared machine learning and deep learning models for predicting real estate asking prices in Vietnam, using a common cleaned dataset and a chronological holdout.

On the held-out test set, boosted trees were the strongest models on structured attributes and outperformed the embedding-based MLP, in line with the strength of tabular baselines reported by \textcite{gorishniy2021revisiting}. Listing text improved every model it was added to, and BamiBERT on text alone outperformed the best structured model. Combining all models gave the lowest MAE (3.659 billion VND, $R^2=0.794$), although most of that gain was already reached by the structured + BamiBERT blend.

These results concern advertised asking prices under a single time-based split, and are subject to the limitations in Section~\ref{subsec:evaluation-limitations}. Further work should check the text for residual price mentions, add property-level identifiers, repeat the evaluation over several time windows, and analyse errors by province, property type and price range. Transaction-verified prices and uncertainty estimates would give a more realistic picture of practical valuation performance.
